Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Who We Are

Licensed.tx is a software platform that helps Texas electricians organize their Texas Electrical Contractor License application. Licensed.tx is not affiliated with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR).

2. Data We Collect

Account information

  • Email address, name, hashed password

Licensing information

  • Eligibility answers, years of experience, business entity information, application status and progress

Documents

  • Files you upload to the document vault (licenses, certificates, EIN letters, etc.)

Payment information

  • Processed by Stripe. We only receive a customer ID and payment status. We never store credit card numbers.

Usage data

  • Pages visited, features used, timestamps, IP address (via Cloudflare infrastructure)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the Platform
  • To personalize your licensing roadmap and eligibility assessment
  • To store and organize your licensing documents
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send renewal reminders and status updates
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve the Platform (aggregated, anonymized analytics only)

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising targeting on external platforms.

4. Third-Party Services

Stripe Payment processing. Privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.
Cloudflare Infrastructure, CDN, R2 storage, D1 database. Privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

We do not share your personal data with any other third parties except as required by law.

5. Document Storage and Security

Documents are stored in Cloudflare R2 and are only accessible by you and Licensed.tx staff for support. We use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and access controls. No system is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained while account is active
  • After account deletion: Personal data deleted within 30 days, except billing records retained 7 years for tax purposes
  • Documents: Deleted immediately upon request or within 30 days of account deletion

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, and export your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at support@licensed.tx. We respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required to operate the Platform (session authentication). No tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes by email or via a notice on the Platform. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: support@licensed.tx
